ACJM COURT APP - HOW TO TEST "DEMAND" AND "LIVE DEPOSITION"
============================================================

Before testing: restart start_windows.bat, then press Ctrl+F5 in every browser.
Use THREE browser windows (or three computers on the office network):
  Window 1 = Judge   (Chrome)
  Window 2 = Staff   (Chrome Incognito, or Edge)
  Window 3 = Advocate (another browser / another PC / mobile)
Each window must be a separate browser or incognito window, because one browser
window keeps only one login at a time.

Test logins
-----------
Judge    : Judge1 (your judge login) - Court No. 1
Staff    : Court1 (staff of Court No. 1 - staff must be of the SAME court as the judge)
Advocate : 9000000001 or advocate1@test.in   password Adv@1234  (Test Advocate One)
Advocate : 9000000002 or advocate2@test.in   password Adv@1234  (Test Advocate Two)

A. START A DEPOSITION WITH A REGISTERED ADVOCATE (Judge window)
  1. Judge Desk > Oral Evidence > Deposition > select a case > New Deposition.
  2. In "Advocate of Producing Party" type "test" - a list of registered advocates
     appears. Click "Test Advocate One". A green "Registered" tag appears.
     (Do the same for Defending Party with "Test Advocate Two" if you like.)
  3. Leave "Vulnerable Witness" UNTICKED. Proceed and start typing.

B. LIVE DEPOSITION (Advocate window)
  1. Home > Advocate > log in as Test Advocate One.
  2. Click the red tile "Live Deposition". The deposition appears marked LIVE.
  3. Open it. Whatever the judge types appears here within 2-3 seconds.
  4. Click "Raise Objection", type an objection, click "Send to Court".
  5. In the Judge window a pop-up shows the objection with a "Copy text" button.
     Copy it, close the pop-up, paste it in the deposition and type the order.
  6. The advocate sees the pasted objection and the order in the live text, and
     under "Your objections" the status changes to "Seen by the Court".
  Checks:
   - Test Advocate Two (not selected / not named) should NOT see it
     (unless he was selected for the other party).
   - A litigant cannot see live depositions.
   - Tick "Vulnerable Witness" (Edit witness details) - the deposition disappears
     from the advocate's list (in camera).
   - Adjourn or Complete the deposition - it immediately stops being visible and
     objections cannot be sent.

C. DEMAND (Judge + Staff windows)
  1. In the Judge typing screen click "Demand". Write which document/portion is
     required (optional) and click "Send Demand".
  2. Staff window (any staff screen): a notification "1 demand from the Court"
     appears within a few seconds with a sound. Click "Open"
     (or use the "Demands from Court" tile).
  3. Staff pastes the OCR text (Ctrl+V) or types it, and clicks "Send to Court".
  4. Judge window: a pop-up shows the text with "Copy text". Copy and paste it
     into the deposition. Earlier texts/objections can be seen again with the
     "Received" button in the bottom bar.
  Checks:
   - After the deposition is Adjourned or Completed, the Demand button is greyed
     out, and any demand still pending ends automatically (the staff screen
     shows it as "Ended automatically").
