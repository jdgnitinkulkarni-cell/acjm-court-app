Today the matter has been placed before this Court.  Heard both the parties and the advocate appearing for both the parties.
 Hearing the parties and scrutinizing the record of the matter, it appears that the matter is pending at the same stage for a considerable period of time, and the complainant of the matter is not taking any interest in the matter to conduct its trial in a time-bound manner.  Considering  the fact thatthe complainant of the matter has wasted a considerable time to produce the evidence, and despite that, neither the complainant nor the advocate of the complainant have remained present during several hearings.

::Order::
 And considering all the above factors, the matter is hereby ordered to be dismissed under Section 279 of BNSS, 2023.
